About

I'm the AI Engagement Lead at KAPI Advisors, where I lead enterprise AI engagements end to end: discovery, solution shaping, delivery planning, UAT, and adoption. I'm the primary point of accountability to client executive sponsors, and I coordinate distributed engineering teams across the US and India to deliver AI agents, digital twins, and enterprise GenAI solutions.

I'm technically fluent enough to challenge enterprise architects on RAG design, agent orchestration, and conversational AI, and I'm just as focused on translating those decisions into business outcomes. I also build the reusable assets (UAT strategies, governance playbooks, executive reporting) that make every engagement start faster than the last.

Before KAPI, I spent over four years at Espressive, most recently as Director of Product Operations (ML), where I scaled the organization from 2 to 14 and owned delivery of enterprise AI agent programs. Earlier, I worked on NLU and speech systems at Google and Apple, and on corpus research at Microsoft Research India.

My foundation is language. I hold a Ph.D. in Linguistics from the University of Southern California and taught NLP-focused courses at UC San Diego. That training still shapes how I think about AI: what people mean, how systems misread them, and how to design for the gap.
